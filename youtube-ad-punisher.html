Punisher

An ad plays. One reload. You land back exactly where you were — with zero waiting and no skip-button hunt.

Get it on the Chrome Web Store →

How it works

  1. Detects the ad overlay the moment it appears on YouTube.
  2. Captures your exact playback position.
  3. Reloads the page with the timestamp in the URL.
  4. YouTube seeks back to your spot and playback resumes.
  5. A short cooldown prevents reload loops; after repeated failures it briefly switches tabs to let the ad state settle.

Privacy

Ad history is stored locally with chrome.storage.local and is never transmitted. You can clear it from the extension popup at any time. Permissions are limited to storage, tabs, and YouTube host access.
